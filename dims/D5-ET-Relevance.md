# D5: ET Relevance (20 pts)

> **Core Question:** How deeply does this problem statement connect to ET's industry focus areas, business context, and data ecosystems? Would solving this feel like "building for ET" — or could it be done identically for any industry hackathon?

This dimension measures specificity to ET's domains. A high score is double-edged: it means the PS is unique and aligned with ET's editorial and business interests (good for selection), but may also mean less prior art from other domains (trade-off with D1).

---

## What We're Measuring

ET relevance isn't binary — it's a spectrum across multiple dimensions:

| Factor | Question |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Industry Focus** | Does the PS address one of ET's core coverage areas (industrial, energy, supply chain, smart cities, cybersecurity, sustainability)? |
| **Business Context** | Is it tied to a key business, policy, or economic theme relevant to ET's audience? |
| **India Context** | Is the problem India-specific (Indian industrial safety, Indian supply chains, Indian cities)? |
| **Data Ecosystem** | Does the PS rely on data sources that ET can provide or facilitate access to? |
| **Data Exclusivity** | Could other generic sources substitute, or is industry-specific data essential? |

## Scoring Guide for D5

### 18–20: ET-Unique

The PS is fundamentally tied to ET's industry and business focus. You cannot do this without specific industry, business, or data context that ET brings.

**Indicators:**
- Primary data sources are from ET's coverage domains or ET-facilitated datasets
- Problem is about a specific Indian industry sector with unique constraints
- Uses India-specific regulatory, policy, or operational context
- The problem was written with ET's audience and business interests in mind

### 14–17: ET-Advantaged

ET/industry data or context is central, but equivalent open data exists as fallback. You could do this with other data, but the ET context is the primary target.

**Indicators:**
- Uses industry data that has open equivalents (e.g., CPCB data for air quality)
- Problem is India-contextualized (Indian regulation, Indian business environment)
- ET has unique access/advantage but isn't the only source
- Could prototype with generic data, but final solution targets ET domains

### 10–13: India-Relevant

The PS addresses an Indian problem using generally available data. The ET connection is in the application domain, not the data source.

**Indicators:**
- Problem is geographically scoped to India
- Uses globally available data applied to Indian context
- ET may have complementary data but it's not the primary source
- "India" is in the PS context but the methodology is universal

### 6–9: Lightly ET-Connected

The ET connection is tangential. The PS could be from any hackathon without changing much.

**Indicators:**
- Uses generic data sources with no specific industry/ET component
- Problem domain has no specific Indian business angle
- ET connection is mostly in the event branding, not the technical challenge
- The same problem appears in non-industry hackathons

### 0–5: No ET Connection

The PS has no meaningful ET connection. Could be run by any organization.

**Indicators:**
- All data, methods, and context are from generic sources
- No Indian-specific element
- Problem is a standard ML/engineering challenge with industry-themed branding

## The ET Uniqueness Multiplier

Some PS score lower on D5 but compensate with stronger D1-D4 (more prior art). Others score high on D5 but lose on D1-D4 (less prior art). The tension is intentional — use this table to weigh the trade-off:

| Scenario | D1-D4 | D5 | Verdict |
| ---------------- | --------------- | --------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Sweet Spot** | High (60+) | High (15+) | Ideal — unique to ET AND lots to borrow from |
| **Safe Bet** | Very High (70+) | Medium (10-14) | Buildable, but less ET-specific — still strong |
| **Passion Play** | Medium (45-59) | Very High (18+) | Hard to build, but deeply ET — high risk/reward |
| **Commodity** | High (60+) | Low (<10) | Easy but generic — may not stand out to ET judges |
| **Avoid** | Low (<45) | Low (<10) | Neither buildable nor ET-specific — skip |

---

## Red Flags

- **PS description never mentions industry or business context** — purely generic
- **All example data and references are from generic sources** — no industry anchoring
- **The problem is a standard Kaggle competition with ET branding** — feels inauthentic

## Green Flags 🟢

- **PS explicitly addresses Indian industry, policy, or business themes** — anchored in ET's coverage
- **ET could provide or facilitate access to relevant datasets** — active support potential
- **The problem addresses a known industry operational need** — real impact
- **The PS references Indian national programs** (Smart Cities, FAME, Digital India, etc.)
